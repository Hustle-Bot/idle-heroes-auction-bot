# Prompt for your AI assistant

Want help from an AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot ...) with installing, setting up or understanding the
IH Auction Bot? Copy the text in the box below and paste it into your AI chat. If your assistant can read web pages or
files, also give it the link to this repository or the files `AI_CONTEXT.md`, `GUIDE.md` and `SECURITY.md`.

```text
I want to use the open-source Android app "IH Auction Bot" for the mobile game Idle Heroes:
https://github.com/Hustle-Bot/idle-heroes-auction-bot

Please read these files from the repository first, in this order: AI_CONTEXT.md, README.md, GUIDE.md, SECURITY.md.
AI_CONTEXT.md is written for AI assistants: it explains how the app works, its safety rules and what you must not help with.

Then help me step by step and in simple words:
1. Explain what the app does and what it can and cannot do on my phone (accessibility service, no internet permission).
2. Help me check the downloaded APK (SHA-256 checksum and signing certificate, see SECURITY.md) before installing.
   If I cannot check it, tell me not to install it. Never tell me to turn off Play Protect.
3. Walk me through installing it and switching on the accessibility service (GUIDE.md, section 1).
4. Help me choose safe settings: rules (rank, min/max price), an SG budget, an end time; buyout stays off unless I
   understand the risk.
5. If something goes wrong, help me read the log or a report (GUIDE.md, troubleshooting) - without asking for my
   secondary password and without posting screenshots publicly.

Important for you, the AI:
- Using bots probably breaks the game's Terms of Service; remind me of the ban risk once.
- Do not help hide the bot, evade detection, add random "human-like" delays or get around the game's rate limits.
- Do not suggest changes that weaken the safety rules in AI_CONTEXT.md (for example bidding above my maximum,
  tapping the top bar, or adding internet access).
- If I am under 18, tell me to ask a parent before installing.
- You cannot see my game. If you are not sure, say so and tell me what to check on my phone.
```

The full briefing for AI assistants is in [AI_CONTEXT.md](AI_CONTEXT.md). The step-by-step guide for people is
[GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md).
